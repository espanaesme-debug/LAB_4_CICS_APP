Degree Programs at UST CICS

Three four-year programs prepare students for distinct roles across the fast-changing computing profession.

Our Programs

BSCS

Computer Science

Focuses on computing theory, algorithms, software engineering, data science, and artificial intelligence.

BSIS

Information Systems

Focuses on business analysis, enterprise systems, project management, and technology-enabled organizations.

BSIT

Information Technology

Focuses on systems administration, networking, cybersecurity, cloud services, and IT operations.
